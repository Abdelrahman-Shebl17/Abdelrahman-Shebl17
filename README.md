## Hey, I'm Abdelrahman Shebl 👋

I'm a **UI/UX Designer** with a Computer Science background, focused on turning complex product requirements, messy flows, and technical constraints into clear and usable digital experiences.

My work sits between **product thinking, UX research, interaction design, systems thinking, and visual craft**.

I enjoy getting into the complicated parts of a product, understanding how everything connects, and simplifying the experience without losing what matters.

Currently, I'm focused on growing as a **Product Designer**, working on real product problems and improving how I connect design decisions with user needs, business goals, and technical constraints.
